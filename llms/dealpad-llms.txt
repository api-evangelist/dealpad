# Dealpad

> Dealpad is an AI-powered buyer-enablement and digital-sales-room platform for B2B sales teams. It centralizes the buyer/seller relationship into shared digital sales rooms, mutual action plans, and content, layering "Sales Mind AI" predictive insight over the deal to help sellers progress complex, multi-stakeholder opportunities. Dealpad integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho CRMs. This profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network; Dealpad does not currently publish a public developer API, OpenAPI, or developer portal.
